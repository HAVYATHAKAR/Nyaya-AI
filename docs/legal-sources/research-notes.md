## Verified official records and attached text

Each official record page and its DSpace bitstream attachment was opened directly. The attachment content exposed the text, title, act number, arrangement/section count, and commencement clause. Each supplied PDF says `[As on the 6th October, 2025]`; that is the version date printed in the document, not proof that it includes amendments after that date. Source retrieval date is 2026-11-27.
